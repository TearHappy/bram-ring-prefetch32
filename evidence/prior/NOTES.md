# Prior measurements (supporting evidence)

All records in this folder were produced on i7-12700KF hosts (Ubuntu 24.04, GCC 13.3) with the
eight physical P-cores pinned, before this package was frozen. They bind to the contestant source with SHA-256
`0bade1287b6775b45c4515c78f4674f3cb4fcdd6c5451058ccddde5209b2182b` (`source-binding-0bade.json`). The source in
this package differs from that file only in comments, output labels, the removal of an unused macro, a split of
the digest helper, and the default benchmark in `main` (which now times the digest and prints the digest fold);
`hash_state`, `build_tables` and `init_heads` are byte-identical. The full diff is
`source-diff-0bade128-to-package.diff`. The package itself was measured with the organizer's literal commands in
the sibling replay folder; the numbers here are supporting evidence, not the claim.

The "digest wrapper" is `private_review/tools/wrapper.c`: both implementations compiled into one independent
timing harness that performs fill, run and the full SHA-256 digest for every timed nonce, compares every digest
and full state between the two, and alternates execution order. Rates are H/s over 800 nonces per run.

Sensitivity rule, stated in advance and applied uniformly: a pair is "quiet" when both of its rates are within 5% of
their column maximum; the quiet-pairs ratio is shown beside the all-pairs ratio and decides nothing. The two
ablation records with few quiet pairs were taken while the shared host was loaded by other tenants (both columns
dropped together); they are retained unedited and support no conclusion about the ablated variants.

Fresh epoch: `fresh-seed-3a824699-vectors.txt` are the eight vectors generated by the unmodified `generate.py`
with `BRAM_EPOCH=3a824699e80b3fd7d46f71ac4dbecae9`; `fresh-seed-3a824699-contestant-rows.txt` are the contestant's
rows for the same seed. All eight rows are identical on nonce, digest, fold and full state.
